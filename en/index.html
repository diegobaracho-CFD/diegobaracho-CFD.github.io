Independent project

Turbulence Modelling Study: URANS versus DDES

Self-directedAnsys Fluent
  • Compared URANS (SST k-ω) with delayed detached-eddy simulation for the flow over a NACA 4412 aerofoil at 14° incidence, just before stall: Re ≈ 9 × 10⁶, 780k cells, max y+ ≈ 1.72.
  • URANS lift and drag coefficients agreed closely with published experimental data, while DDES overpredicted them by 30% to 40%, a discrepancy I attributed to mesh resolution insufficient for a scale-resolving method within the one-million-cell limit of the Ansys Student licence. Written up as a technical post on LinkedIn.
