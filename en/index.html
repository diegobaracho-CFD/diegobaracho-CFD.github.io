Professional Profile

Aerospace Engineering student specialising in computational fluid dynamics, with hands-on experience in finite-volume simulation, mesh generation and quality assessment, turbulence modelling and experimental validation in a wind tunnel. Research & Computational Fluid Dynamics

CFD Study: URANS versus DDES

Turbulence Modelling Study on the NACA 4412 Aerofoil Self-directed · Ansys Fluent
Reynolds Number Re ≈ 9 × 10⁶
Computational Grid 780k cells
Maximum y+ ≈ 1.72
Angle of Attack α = 14°

Compared URANS (SST k-ω) with delayed detached-eddy simulation (DDES) for the flow over a NACA 4412 aerofoil at 14° incidence, just before stall.

URANS

URANS lift (CL) and drag (CD) coefficients agreed closely with published experimental data.

DDES

DDES overpredicted them by 30% to 40%, a discrepancy I attributed to mesh resolution insufficient for a scale-resolving method within the one-million-cell limit of the Ansys Student licence. Written up as a technical post on LinkedIn.
